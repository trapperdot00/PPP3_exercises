The free store is unused memory that can be
allocated by programs at run-time.

The free store is also called the heap.

To allocate and construct objects on the heap
in one operation, one could use:
 - new   for singular objects
 - new[] for arrays of objects

To allocate raw, uninitialized memory on the heap,
one could use:
 - operator new
 - operator new[]
 - malloc
 - calloc

To construct objects in uninitialized memory:
 - placement new

To destroy and deallocate objects from the heap
in one operation, one could use:
 - delete   for singular objects
 - delete[] for arrays of objects

To destroy objects, call their destructors explicitly.

To deallocate already destroyed objects from the heap,
one could use:
 - operator delete
 - operator delete[]
 - free
